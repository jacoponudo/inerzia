1. PURPOSE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The app runs an online experiment on how people choose and evaluate news.
Participants browse short news feeds and are free to open, read and like
articles. In a first phase every participant sees which outlet published each
article (through a colour). In a second phase the source cue and/or the
original wording are manipulated between subjects. The app records every
interaction so the preference gap between outlets can be compared across the
two phases.

The whole interface is in English.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STUDY DESIGN AS IMPLEMENTED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stimuli
  - 32 news articles shared on Reddit before 2022.
  - 4 topics (e.g. Covid, Politics, Climate, Economy).
  - 4 outlets: 2 reliable and 2 unreliable.
  - Each outlet contributes 2 articles per topic (4 topics x 4 outlets x 2 =
    32 articles).
  - Every article exists in two versions: the original text and a version
    rewritten by an LLM.

Phases
  Phase 1 - baseline (16 articles, pages 1-4)
    One page per topic. Each page shows 4 articles, one per outlet. The source
    colour is always visible and the text is always the original.

  Phase 2 - treatment (the remaining 16 articles, pages 5-8)
    Same structure, with the other article of each outlet for each topic. The
    participant's condition decides what is shown.

  There is no break, transition screen or announcement between the phases:
  page 5 follows page 4 exactly as page 2 follows page 1.

Conditions (between subjects, Phase 2 only)

                        | Source visible (colour) | Source hidden (no colour)
    --------------------+-------------------------+--------------------------
    Original text       | Condition 1             | Condition 2
    LLM-rewritten text  | Condition 3             | Condition 4

  Condition numbers and labels saved in the data:
    1 = "source visible / original text"
    2 = "source hidden / original text"
    3 = "source visible / rewritten text"
    4 = "source hidden / rewritten text"

  Participants are never told their condition.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PARTICIPANT FLOW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Screen 1 - Instructions and consent
  - Title, a short description of the task and the estimated duration.
  - "How it works", with a small visual sample of each element:
      * "... Read more" link: each article opens with a headline and a
        short preview; Read more keeps reading; after that, Read full article
        shows the whole text.
      * The four colour markers: the coloured marker in the top-left corner
        shows which news outlet published the article; each colour is a
        different outlet.
      * Like button: click to like, click again to undo.
      * Continue button: moves to the next feed; no going back.
  - A neutral note that reading, liking and skipping are all equally useful.
    This replaces the old in-feed message ("Liberta di scelta..."). Nothing
    like it appears while participants read.
  - A participation/consent box (voluntary participation, what is recorded,
    contact and ethics placeholders).
  - A consent checkbox. The "Start the study" button stays disabled until it
    is ticked.

  The session ID and the condition are NOT shown to the participant.

Screens 2-9 - Feed pages (8 pages)
  - Progress indicator: "Page n of 8" with a thin progress bar.
  - Four article cards in random order.
  - A "Continue" button (the last page says "Finish"), always enabled:
    participants can move on without interacting with anything.

Screen 10 - End
  - "Thank you" and confirmation that responses were saved.
  - The Prolific completion code (if configured) and a "Return to Prolific"
    button.
  - If the upload to Google Sheets fails after three attempts, a short message
    and a "Try again" button appear. The completion code is still shown.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. RANDOMISATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Everything below is drawn independently for each participant when they click
"Start the study", using Python's SystemRandom. Every draw is saved in the
"sessions" sheet so the exact stimulus sequence can be reconstructed.

  a. Condition (1-4): balanced assignment, see section 9.
  b. Colour mapping: the 4 palette colours are shuffled and assigned to the
     4 outlets. The mapping stays the same for the whole session (Phase 1
     and Phase 2). Saved as color_map.
  c. Phase split: for each topic x outlet, one of the 2 articles is drawn for
     Phase 1 and the other goes to Phase 2. Saved as article_split.
  d. Topic order in Phase 1: random permutation of the 4 topics.
  e. Topic order in Phase 2: a second random permutation, independent of d.
     Saved as phase1_topic_order and phase2_topic_order.
  f. Article order within each page: random permutation of the 4 articles
     (one per outlet), drawn separately for each of the 8 pages. Saved per
     article as "position" (1 = top of the feed).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE FEED PAGE AND THE ARTICLE CARD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Card anatomy (top to bottom)
  - Colour marker: a small bookmark-shaped ribbon in the outlet's colour,
    attached to the top-left edge of the card. It carries the outlet colour
    only: no outlet name or domain is shown anywhere.
    When the source is hidden the marker is removed entirely (no grey
    placeholder).
  - Headline.
  - Text, revealed in three stages (see below).
  - Like button.

Text stages ("read more" pattern, after the WebMarcello tutorial)
  The reveal follows the inline "...read more" pattern of the referenced
  tutorial: the link sits inline right after the last visible word.

  Stage 0 - preview:   the first 2 sentences, then the inline link
                       "... Read more".
  Stage 1 - read more: the text grows to 5 sentences in total (the 2 already
                       shown + 3 new ones), then the inline link
                       "... Read full article".
                       "Read full article" only exists after "Read more"
                       has been clicked.
  Stage 2 - full:      the whole article.

  - There is no "Read less": once opened, a text stays open.
  - Newly revealed sentences fade in briefly so readers see what was added.
  - Paragraph breaks of the source text are preserved: any line break in the
    CSV text starts a new paragraph.
  - Edge cases: if an article has 2 sentences or fewer, no link is shown
    (readmore_available = FALSE). If it has 3-5 sentences, "Read more"
    shows everything and "Read full article" never appears
    (full_available = FALSE). Both flags are saved so these cases can be
    excluded or handled in the analysis.
  - The sentence counts are set by SNIPPET_SENTENCES (2) and
    READMORE_SENTENCES (5) in app.py.

Sentence splitting
  Text is split into sentences after ".", "!", "?" or "..." (optionally
  followed by a closing quote or bracket) when the next word starts with a
  capital letter or a digit. Common abbreviations (Mr., Dr., U.S., U.K.,
  e.g., Jan., etc.) and single initials ("J. K. Rowling") are protected so
  they do not end a sentence. See section 17 for the limits of this rule.

Like
  - A single thumbs-up button that toggles on and off. It is optional.
  - Only its state when the participant leaves the page counts
    (liked_final). Every toggle is also logged with a timestamp
    (like_history).

Navigation
  - "Continue" saves the page's interactions in memory and loads the next
    page, scrolled to the top.
  - There is no "Back" button: each page's data are final when the
    participant clicks Continue.
  - While the study is in progress the browser asks for confirmation if the
    participant tries to close or reload the tab (WARN_ON_LEAVE). The guard
    is removed when "Finish" is clicked.

6. WHAT PARTICIPANTS SEE IN EACH PHASE AND CONDITION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                   Pages 1-4 (Phase 1)       Pages 5-8 (Phase 2)
    Condition 1    colour + original text    colour + original text
    Condition 2    colour + original text    no colour + original text
    Condition 3    colour + original text    colour + rewritten text
    Condition 4    colour + original text    no colour + rewritten text

Headlines in the rewritten conditions: if the CSV has a rewritten-headline
column (title_rewritten), it is used. Otherwise the original headline is
shown and only the body text is rewritten (see section 18).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DATA RECORDED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Three worksheets are used in the Google Sheet. They are created
automatically, with their header row, on first use.

7.1 Sheet "responses" - one row per article per participant (32 rows each)

  session_id              Unique session ID (UTC timestamp + 6 random chars).
  prolific_pid            Prolific participant ID from the URL (empty if none).
  condition               1-4.
  condition_label         Text label of the condition.
  phase                   1 or 2.
  page_number             1-8, in the order seen.
  page_in_phase           1-4.
  topic                   Topic of the page.
  position                Position of the card in the feed (1 = top).
  article_uid             Article ID ("id" column of the CSV if present,
                          otherwise "a" + row number, e.g. a07).
  outlet                  Outlet/domain of the article.
  reliability_class       reliable / unreliable (see section 11).
  outlet_color            Colour assigned to the outlet for this participant
                          (saved even when the colour was hidden).
  source_visible          TRUE if the colour marker was shown.
  text_version            original / rewritten.
  title_shown             Headline actually displayed.
  n_sentences             Number of sentences in the displayed text.
  readmore_available      FALSE if the text was too short to need it.
  readmore_clicked        TRUE if "Read more" was clicked.
  readmore_ms             Milliseconds from page load to the click.
  readmore_ts             Click time, ISO 8601 UTC (participant's clock).
  full_available          FALSE if "Read more" already showed everything.
  full_clicked            TRUE if "Read full article" was clicked.
  full_ms                 Milliseconds from page load to the click.
  full_ts                 Click time, ISO 8601 UTC.
  liked_final             Like state when the participant left the page.
  like_toggles            Number of Like clicks.
  like_history            JSON list of every toggle:
                          [{"liked": true, "ms": 5120, "ts": "..."}, ...]
  page_enter_ts           Time the page appeared (participant's clock).
  page_exit_ts            Time "Continue" was clicked.
  page_duration_ms        Time on the page, measured in the browser.
  page_duration_server_s  Time on the page measured by the server (backup).
  year, month, rating     Copied from the CSV, for convenience.

  Notes
  - Time on page is recorded per page, not per article. With four articles
    on one screen, per-article reading time cannot be separated reliably;
    the click timestamps give the order and timing of expansions.
  - "Interactions" for the preference gap can be computed from
    readmore_clicked, full_clicked and liked_final, by outlet and phase.

7.2 Sheet "sessions" - one row per participant who completed the study

  session_id, prolific_pid, study_id, prolific_session_id
  condition, condition_label
  assignment_method       balanced | random_no_storage | random_storage_error
                          | forced_dev
  color_map               JSON {outlet: colour}
  phase1_topic_order      JSON list of topics in the order shown
  phase2_topic_order      JSON list
  article_split           JSON {"1": {topic: [ids]}, "2": {topic: [ids]}}
  opened_at_utc           First load of the app.
  consent_at_utc          Click on "Start the study".
  started_at_utc          Same moment (randomisation done).
  completed_at_utc        Click on "Finish".
  duration_min            Minutes from start to the end screen.
  n_articles, n_readmore, n_full, n_likes   Totals for quick checks.
  user_agent              Browser/device string.
  app_version             Version of app.py that produced the data.

7.3 Sheet "assignments" - one row per participant who clicked Start

  session_id, prolific_pid, condition, assigned_at_utc

  This row contains no responses. It only reserves a place in a condition
  so that participants starting at the same time are spread across
  conditions (section 9). Comparing it with "sessions" gives the drop-out
  rate per condition.

7.4 Local backup
  At the end, the app also writes data/responses/<session_id>_responses.csv
  and <session_id>_session.json next to app.py. On Streamlit Community Cloud
  the local disk is wiped at every restart, so this is only a safety net:
  Google Sheets is the real data store.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. SAVING AND STORAGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - All interaction data are kept in the participant's session and written
    once, when the end screen opens (responses first, then the session
    row).
  - If a participant leaves before finishing, none of their responses are
    saved (as stated in the consent text). Only their "assignments" row
    exists.
  - The upload is retried up to 3 times with increasing waits. Each part is
    written only once, so a retry never duplicates rows.
  - If all attempts fail, the end screen shows "Try again"; the completion
    code is still shown so the participant can be paid. The failure is
    printed in the app logs.
  - If Google Sheets is not configured (e.g. local testing), only the local
    backup is written and conditions are drawn at random.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. BALANCED ASSIGNMENT TO CONDITIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Goal: equal numbers of COMPLETED participants per condition (block
randomisation counting completers only).

When a participant clicks "Start the study":
  1. The app counts, for each condition:
       - participants who completed the study ("sessions" sheet), plus
       - participants who started less than PENDING_WINDOW_MIN minutes ago
         (default 60) and have not finished yet ("assignments" sheet).
  2. The new participant is assigned to the condition with the lowest count.
     Ties are broken at random.
  3. A row is added to "assignments".

Why step 1 includes recent starters: Prolific often sends many people at
once. If only completers counted, everyone starting in the same minutes would
get the same condition. Counting recent starters spreads a burst evenly.
Once a reservation is older than the window and has no completed session, it
stops counting, so drop-outs free their place. The result keeps completed
counts within about one participant of each other (tested: 40 sequential
completers -> 10/10/10/10; a burst of 12 simultaneous starts -> 3/3/3/3).

Set PENDING_WINDOW_MIN comfortably above the typical completion time, but not
much more, or drop-outs will hold places too long.

If Google Sheets cannot be reached at that moment, the condition is drawn at
random and assignment_method records it.

10. PROLIFIC INTEGRATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - The app reads PROLIFIC_PID, STUDY_ID and SESSION_ID from the URL.
    In Prolific, choose "I'll use URL parameters" so the study URL becomes:
      https://<your-app>.streamlit.app/?PROLIFIC_PID={{%PROLIFIC_PID%}}&STUDY_ID={{%STUDY_ID%}}&SESSION_ID={{%SESSION_ID%}}
  - Completion: put the Prolific completion code in secrets.toml
    ([prolific] completion_code). The end screen shows it and the
    "Return to Prolific" button opens:
      https://app.prolific.com/submissions/complete?cc=<code>
    The button opens in a new tab, which works even though Streamlit Cloud
    serves apps inside a frame.
  - The app runs without a Prolific ID (useful for testing); prolific_pid is
    then empty.

11. INPUT FILE (ARTICLES CSV)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Location:  data/articoli_selezionati - articoli scelti.csv (next to app.py)

Columns (for each field the app uses the first name it finds; edit
COLUMN_CANDIDATES in app.py if yours differ):

  Field             Accepted column names                       Required
  ----------------  ------------------------------------------  --------
  id                id, article_id                              no
  topic             topic                                       yes
  outlet            domain, outlet, source                      yes
  title             title, headline                             no*
  title_rewritten   title_rewritten, rewritten_title,           no
                    headline_rewritten
  text              clean_text, text                            yes
  text_rewritten    rewritten_text, text_rewritten,             yes
                    clean_text_rewritten, llm_text, ai_text,
                    rewritten
  reliability       reliability_class, reliability_label        no
  rating            rating                                      no
  year, month       year, month                                 no

  * Without a headline column, cards show the text only.

Reliability class
  - From the reliability column if present.
  - Otherwise derived from "rating": the outlets are ranked by mean rating
    and the top half are labelled "reliable", the bottom half "unreliable".
  - Otherwise "NA".

Validation (checked when the app loads)
  - Required columns exist.
  - Every topic x outlet combination has exactly 2 articles.
  - No more outlets than palette colours (4).
  - No empty original or rewritten text, no duplicate IDs.
  If anything fails, the app shows "The study isn't set up correctly" with
  the list of problems instead of the study. Check this before launching.
  Editing the CSV reloads it automatically on the next page load.

     automatically.

13.6 Streamlit Community Cloud

  1. Push the project to a GitHub repository. A private repository is
     recommended, since it contains the stimuli.
  2. On share.streamlit.io, create an app from the repository with app.py
     as the main file.
  3. In the app's settings, paste the content of secrets.toml into
     "Secrets".
  4. Open the app once and complete it yourself to check that rows appear
     in the Google Sheet. Then delete your test rows from all three sheets
     before launching, so they don't affect the balancing.
  5. Free Streamlit apps go to sleep after a period without visitors: open
     the app just before publishing the Prolific study.

13.7 Running locally

  pip install -r requirements.txt
  streamlit run app.py

14. TESTING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The app was tested end to end in a headless browser with a synthetic 32-row
CSV (the real CSV had not been shared yet):
  - Instructions -> consent -> 8 pages -> end screen, desktop (1280 px) and
    mobile (390 px) widths.
  - 32 different articles per participant, 16 per phase, 4 topics per
    phase, one article per outlet on every page.
  - Colour mapping identical across phases; different across participants.
  - Condition 4 forced: markers present on pages 1-4, absent on pages 5-8,
    rewritten text on pages 5-8.
  - Read more / full article clicks, like toggles (on/off) and timings
    recorded correctly in the saved file.
  - Balanced assignment and the Google Sheets writing logic, with a
    simulated spreadsheet (results in section 9).
  - Prolific completion code and button on the end screen.
Not tested: a real Google Sheet (needs your credentials) and the real CSV.

How to test yourself
  - Set DEV_MODE = True and open the app with ?condition=1 (2, 3, 4) to see
    each condition. Set it back to False before launch.
  - Add ?PROLIFIC_PID=TEST to the URL to check the ID is saved.

15. VISUAL DESIGN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Principle: colour is data. In this experiment the outlet colour is the
manipulated cue, so it is the only colour on screen. All other elements -
text, links, buttons, the active Like state, the progress bar - use neutral
greys and near-black. That is why the "Read more" link is bold dark text
with an underline instead of the tutorial's blue: a blue link could be
confused with the blue outlet colour.

  - Background: cool light grey (#ECEEF1). Cards: white with a thin grey
    border, no shadows.
  - Typefaces (Google Fonts): Schibsted Grotesk for headlines and interface
    (a grotesque designed for a news publisher), Literata for article text
    (a serif designed for long on-screen reading).
  - The colour marker is a bookmark-shaped ribbon hanging from the top-left
    edge of the card: the one strong visual element, easy to learn as a
    label of the source.
  - The only motion responds to participant actions: newly revealed
    sentences fade in, the Like icon gives a small press feedback, and the
    progress bar advances between pages. Motion is turned off for
    participants who have "reduce motion" enabled.
  - Single centred column (max 700 px), readable line lengths, works down
    to phone width (the Continue button becomes full width). Visible
    keyboard focus on all controls.
  - Streamlit's own header, menu, footer and "running" indicator are
    hidden, so the study looks like a standalone site.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Streamlit handles the screens, the session state, randomisation,
    storage and Prolific.
  - Each feed page is a custom HTML/JavaScript component embedded in
    Streamlit (FEED_COMPONENT_HTML in app.py; the file is written to a
    temporary folder at start-up, so app.py stays a single file).
  - All interactions on a page (read more, full article, like) happen in the
    browser with no reload, so they are instant and timestamps are precise.
    When "Continue" is clicked, the component sends one JSON object with
    the page's interactions back to Python, which stores it and shows the
    next page.
  - Article text is inserted as plain text, never as HTML, so content in the
    CSV cannot break or inject code into the page.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
17. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Reloading or closing the tab ends the session. Reopening the link starts
    a new session with a new ID, new randomisation and possibly a new
    condition; the abandoned attempt is not saved. The leave-confirmation
    prompt reduces this. Repeated Prolific IDs in "assignments" reveal it.
  - The sentence splitter is rule-based. An abbreviation at the end of a
    sentence followed by a capitalised word ("...in the U.S. Officials
    said") is not split. Sentences starting with a lowercase letter are
    joined to the previous one. Check a few articles on screen once the
    real CSV is loaded.
  - Two participants clicking Start in the same second can occasionally get
    the same condition; the imbalance corrects itself with later
    participants.
  - Google Sheets has per-minute request quotas. Each participant uses
    about 3 requests at start and 2 at the end. For very large bursts,
    publish in batches on Prolific. If the quota is hit at start, the
    condition falls back to random (recorded in assignment_method); at the
    end the upload is retried.
  - Fonts load from Google Fonts. If blocked, system fonts are used and the
    layout still works.
  - Click times (readmore_ts, full_ts, page_*_ts) come from the
    participant's computer clock; the *_ms durations do not depend on it.

19. CHANGES FROM THE PREVIOUS VERSION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Single-article pages replaced by topic pages with a 4-article feed.
  - Topics and articles now follow the design (8 pages; one article per
    outlet per page; phase split, topic order and article order randomised
    per participant).
  - The treatment now applies to Phase 2 only; Phase 1 is the same for all.
  - Unbalanced random.randint condition replaced by balanced assignment.
  - Colours: fixed colour-blind-safe palette, randomly mapped to outlets per
    participant, shown as a marker at the top-left of each card. Outlet
    names are no longer shown.
  - Expansion: progressive "Mostra altro" in steps of 3 sentences replaced
    by the inline "... Read more" (5 sentences) and then
    "... Read full article", which only appears after Read more.
  - The reaction is no longer required: no "please leave a reaction"
    message, no Skip button, Continue always available.
  - The in-feed "Liberta di scelta" message was removed; instructions are
    given once at the start.
  - The topic name is not shown as a headline any more (the old code used
    the topic as the headline).
  - Condition name, session ID and file paths are no longer shown to
    participants.
  - Whole interface in English.
  - Data saved to Google Sheets (plus local backup) with a much richer log:
    positions, colours, timestamps, like history, time on page.
  - Prolific ID capture and completion redirect.
  - New visual design (section 15).
